Spoken Prepositions are Optional

In spoken English, the preposition "on" is often omitted before the day.

Combining Days and Times

Monday
🌙

Place the time after the day and omit prepositions (e.g., Monday night).

The "Noon and Midnight" Exception

at noon and at midnight

Unlike other times, noon and midnight still require "at" when combined with days.

Time of Day Preposition to Use
Morning, Afternoon, Evening in the
Noon, Midnight, Night at
Weekday, Weekend on / during
