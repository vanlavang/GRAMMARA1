Mastering Yes/No Questions with "Be" Verbs

A clear guide on forming and answering Yes/No questions in Present Simple.

Singular Subjects

🙋‍♂️
I
Am I...?
Yes, you are.
No, I'm not.
🫵
You
Are you...?
Yes, I am.
No, I'm not.
👦👧
He/She/It
Is he/she...?
Yes, he is.
No, he isn't.

Plural Subjects

The Universal "Are"
Use "Are" for ALL plural subjects, including We, They, and plural You.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦🤝👥
🙌
We
Are we...?
👥
They
Are they...?
🗣️
You (all)
Are you...?
Subject Positive Answer Negative Answer
👥 They ✔ Yes, they are. ✖ No, they aren't.
🙌 We ✔ Yes, we are. ✖ No, we aren't.
🗣️ You (Plural) ✔ Yes, we are. ✖ No, we aren't.
