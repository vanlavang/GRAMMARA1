Core Usages & Mechanics
👦 🍕
Adjective
I am hungry.
👩‍🏫 🏫
Noun
She is a teacher.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 🏡
Preposition
We are at home.
Rule: Use 'Be' verbs to link the subject to nouns, adjectives, or prepositions to describe identity, qualities, or locations.
Contractions & Questions
Contractions (Short Forms)
I am
→
I'm
She is
→
She's
They are
→
They're
Used primarily in spoken English and informal writing to combine subjects and verbs seamlessly.
Forming Yes/No Questions
You are
⇄
Are you...?
He is
⇄
Is he...?
Invert the subject and the "Be" verb to construct interrogative forms.
Forming Wh- Questions
[Wh-] +
is she?
are they?
am I?
Place the Question Word (Who, What, Where) at the beginning, followed by the inverted verb structure.

Cơ chế áp dụng & Ví dụ
👦 🍕
Tính từ
I am hungry.
(Tôi đói)
👩‍🏫 🏫
Danh từ
She is a teacher.
(Cô ấy là giáo viên)
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 🏡
Giới từ
We are at home.
(Chúng tôi ở nhà)
Quy tắc: Sử dụng động từ 'Be' trước danh từ, tính từ hoặc giới từ để miêu tả định danh, tính chất hoặc vị trí.
